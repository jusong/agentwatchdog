FAQ

Are the $ numbers my actual bill?

They're close estimates — tokens × current model pricing (cache read/write included). Subscription quota is metered by Anthropic, not dollars; we translate both into a $-denominated budget so your breaker works the same way across plans.

Cursor / other agents?

v1 guards Claude Code (local sessions, hooks, cron). Cursor background agents run in the cloud, so v1.x ships billing-anomaly notifications for them; full interception is on the roadmap.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Parsing happens on your machine; the only network call is the macOS notification.

What happens when the breaker trips?

You get a notification, the menubar turns red, a stop marker is written, and registered hooks deny every further tool call until you hit "Reset & rearm". Fail-open by design — if the watchdog itself fails, your agents keep running.

Can it block legitimate work, or miss a real runaway?

Both, in principle — and we say so plainly. Loop detection uses exact-argument matching, so heavy-but-legitimate repetition can trip it (one click re-arms; every trip shows its reason). And estimates can drift from your actual bill. AgentWatchdog is a safety net, not a guarantee: it reduces the blast radius of rogue agents, it does not promise zero incidents. If it ever blocks work you needed or misses a burn you expected it to catch, that's what the 14-day refund is for.
